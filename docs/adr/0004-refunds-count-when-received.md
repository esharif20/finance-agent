# Refunds count in the month the money comes back

A Refund reduces its Category in the month it is received, and is linked to the original purchase when the two can be matched. Rewriting the purchase month would be more "accurate" per item, but it would change past months' totals after the fact and stop reports reconciling against bank statements. A Category can therefore show negative spending in a month with a large refund; that is intended.
